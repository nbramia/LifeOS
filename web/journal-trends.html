Felt vs. Recorded Connection

Loading…
In-person connection leaves no digital trace, so a day marked "connected" with zero recorded interactions is not an error — it's information about channel, not accuracy. The more useful signal runs the other way: a day not marked as connecting that has recorded messages, meaning a real exchange happened through a channel the self-report didn't credit. Rows like that are flagged below rather than treated as agreement/disagreement.
